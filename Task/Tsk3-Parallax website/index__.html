About the Studio

Design is how we think,
not just how it looks

Founded in 2017, we built Forma on a simple belief: that the best design emerges from genuine understanding. We don't rush to concepts—we spend time with problems, unraveling complexity until clarity appears.

Our team of twelve spans designers, strategists, and developers. We're small enough to stay nimble, experienced enough to know what works. Every project gets our full attention, not just our latest template.

Our Approach

A process built on conversation

01

Listen & Learn

We start every engagement by listening—really listening. To your team, your customers, your competitors. Understanding the landscape before drawing a single wireframe.

02

Define & Refine

Insights become direction. We distill findings into clear problems to solve, measurable goals to hit, and design principles that will guide every decision.

03

Create & Iterate

Ideas take shape through sketches, prototypes, and tests. We iterate quickly, incorporating feedback early when change is cheap and learning is fresh.

04

Launch & Evolve

Great work doesn't end at launch. We monitor, measure, and refine—ensuring your investment continues to deliver value long after the unveiling.
